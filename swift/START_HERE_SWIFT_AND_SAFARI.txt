SWIFT + SAFARI — Anatomy Ballistics (iPad)

Two ways to play the SAME game. Pick one.

═══════════════════════════════════════
OPTION A — Swift Playgrounds (Swift app)
═══════════════════════════════════════
1. Install free “Swift Playgrounds” from the App Store.
2. Open folder:  1_SwiftPlaygrounds / AnatomyBallistics.swiftpm
3. Tap → Open in Swift Playgrounds (or Share → Swift Playgrounds).
4. Press Run (▷) if needed.
5. Tap TAP TO PLAY → drag aim → tap FIRE.

═══════════════════════════════════════
OPTION B — Safari (just open the file)
═══════════════════════════════════════
1. Open folder:  2_Safari
2. Tap OPEN_IN_SAFARI.html
3. Open in Safari.
4. Tap TAP TO PLAY → drag aim → tap FIRE.

CONTROLS
• Drag   = aim (red reticle)
• FIRE   = shoot .22 LR (3 second cooldown)
• NEXT   = next distance / wall stage
• RESET  = rebuild body, restart at 40 ft

STAGES: 40 ft → 15 ft, then house wall.
