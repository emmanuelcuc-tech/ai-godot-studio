Anatomy Ballistics — OPEN IN SAFARI (just play)

1. Save OPEN_IN_SAFARI.html (or index.html) to the Files app on your iPad.
2. Tap the file → Open in Safari (or Share → Safari).
3. Tap TAP TO PLAY.
4. Drag the red reticle to aim.
5. Tap the red FIRE button (or double-tap the screen).
6. Use NEXT for closer ranges / wall stage. RESET to restart.

No Codea. No Python. No App Store purchase. Safari only.

If the file opens as text instead of the game:
- Long-press the file in Files → Share → Copy to Safari
- Or AirDrop the .html to your iPad and choose Safari
