PLAY IN SAFARI (iPad) — Anatomy Ballistics

1. Download PLAY_IN_SAFARI.html  (same as OPEN_IN_SAFARI.html)
2. Save it in the Files app
3. Tap the file
4. Share → Open in Safari   (or “Copy to Safari”)
5. Tap TAP TO PLAY
6. Drag the red reticle to aim
7. Tap the red FIRE button

NEXT = next stage (closer / wall)
RESET = start over

No Swift Playgrounds needed. No Codea. Just Safari.
